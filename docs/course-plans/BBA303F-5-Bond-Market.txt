CIA I Details:
Course Objectives         CO1: Explain the structure and functioning of the bond market in
                          India.
                          CO2: Demonstrate techniques for bond valuation and risk assessment.
Assignment Details        Indian Bond Market Dynamics and Bond Valuation Analytics: A
                          Comparative Study of Government and Corporate Bonds
Assignment                This assignment requires students to analyze the structure and
description               functioning of the Indian Bond Market with reference to Government
                          Securities (G-Secs) and Corporate Bonds using real-time data from
                          the last 5–10 years. Students are expected to study the functioning of
                          primary and secondary bond markets, auction mechanisms, repo
                          markets, and bond valuation techniques.
                          The assignment should include practical application of bond valuation
                          concepts such as Time Value of Money (TVM), Yield to Maturity
                          (YTM), Duration, Convexity, and risk analysis. Students must
                          interpret bond market trends using data from RBI, SEBI, NSE, BSE,
                          CCIL, or other reliable financial databases.
                          Students should critically evaluate how interest rate movements,
                          inflation, and liquidity conditions influence bond prices and
                          yields. They should also discuss the contribution of efficient
                          bond markets toward economic growth and financial market
                          stability, linking their analysis with SDG 8 – Decent Work and
                          Economic Growth.
Submission mode           Moodle
Submission deadline
Individual/Group          Individual
Assignment
Submission                Select one Government Bond and one Corporate Bond listed in
Guidelines                India for comparative analysis.
                               Provide a brief justification for selecting the bonds,
                                  highlighting market relevance, maturity profile, and investor
                                  importance.
                               Collect and analyze historical data related to:
                                       o Bond prices
                                       o Coupon rates
                                       o Yield movements
                                       o RBI auction data
                                       o Repo rate trends
                               Apply at least two bond valuation or risk assessment
                                  techniques learned in Units 1 and 2.
                               Demonstrate interpretation of auction mechanisms and market
                                  behavior.
                               Use graphs, yield trend charts, duration tables, and valuation
                                  illustrations for better clarity.
                               Include proper citations, references, and data sources.

Assignment Details    Sustainable and Digital Bond Markets: Innovations, Green
                      Finance, and the Future of Fixed Income Securities
Assignment            This group presentation requires students to explore emerging
description           trends and innovations transforming global and Indian bond
                      markets, with special focus on sustainability-linked bonds, green
                      bonds, digital bonds, tokenized securities, and fintech-enabled
                      bond analytics.
                      Students are expected to critically analyze how technological
                      innovations such as blockchain, fintech platforms, AI-driven
                      analytics, and tokenization are reshaping the bond market
                      ecosystem. The presentation should also examine the role of
                      sustainable finance instruments in supporting environmental and
                      economic objectives aligned with the United Nations
                      Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs).
                      Each group must evaluate recent developments in India and
                      compare them with global practices in sustainable and digital
                      bond markets. Students should use current market data, industry
                      reports, RBI/SEBI guidelines, and case studies to demonstrate
                      analytical understanding and practical relevance.
                      The work should reflect conceptual clarity, teamwork, analytical
                      thinking, presentation effectiveness, and linkage to SDG 9
                      (Industry, Innovation and Infrastructure) and SDG 8
                      (Decent Work and Economic Growth).
