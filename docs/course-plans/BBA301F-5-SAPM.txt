Course        This course provides students with a comprehensive understanding of
Description   investment strategies and portfolio construction. It covers basic investment
              concepts and types of financial instruments. The course then delves into
              security analysis, including both fundamental and technical analysis methods
              for evaluating stocks, bonds, and other assets. Students will learn security
              pricing and valuation techniques to determine the true value of investments.
              The course further explores portfolio models and analysis, emphasizing
              optimal portfolio construction and risk-return trade-offs. It also covers
              portfolio evaluation, focusing on performance measurement and techniques for
              assessing portfolio success. This course equips students with the skills needed
              to analyze securities, construct diversified portfolios, and evaluate investment
              performance effectively.

Course        This course will help the learner to
Objectives       1. Understand key investment concepts and financial instruments
                 2. Apply fundamental and technical analysis techniques to evaluate
                     securities.
                 3. Analyze different methods of security pricing and valuation to
                     determine the intrinsic value of various investment instruments.
                 4. Construct optimal portfolios using portfolio theory and models
                 5. Evaluate the success of investment portfolios by applying appropriate
                     performance measurement tools

Course        On having completed this course student should be able to:
Outcomes      CLO1 Explain the key investment concepts, financial markets, and various
              financial instruments used in investment decisions.
              CLO2 Apply fundamental and technical analysis techniques to assess and
              evaluate securities in different market situations.
              CLO3 Analyze security pricing and valuation methods to determine the
              intrinsic value of investment instruments
              CLO4 Construct optimal investment portfolios using portfolio theories and
              portfolio management models.
              CLO5 Evaluate the performance and success of investment portfolios using
              appropriate performance measurement techniques.

Assignment Details           Investment Behaviour and Emerging Investment Avenues in India


Assignment description              Students shall undertake a practical and analytical study on
                                    investment behaviour and emerging investment avenues in India by
                                    interacting with investors and relating their observations to concepts
                                    learned in class.
                                    Task Requirements:
                                    Part A: Investor Interaction
                                    Interview any two investors from different backgrounds such as:
                                •   Salary earner
                                •   Trader
                                •   Retired person
                                •   Entrepreneur
                                •   Student
                                •   Business person

                                    Students should collect information regarding:
                                •   Preferred investment avenues
                                •   Risk perception and return expectations
                                •   Tax considerations influencing investment decisions
                                •   Attitude towards speculation and emerging investments such as
                                    cryptocurrencies
                                •   Awareness of financial markets and investment instruments
                                    Part B: Literature Review and AI-Based Comparative Analysis

                                    Prepare a literature review on "Investment Behaviour and
                                    Emerging Investment Avenues." The review should cover investor
                                    behaviour, behavioural biases, and emerging investment avenues
                                    such as ESG investments, REITs, InvITs, ETFs, thematic funds,
                                    fintech-enabled investments, and other contemporary investment
                                    opportunities.
                                     The review must be based on at least 10 scholarly sources, including
                                     peer-reviewed journal articles, books, and credible industry reports.

                                     Use an AI tool (e.g., ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude, or similar) to
                                     conduct a comparative analysis of the investment perceptions and
                                     behaviour of people belonging to the same demographic group in
                                     at least two different countries.
                                     Compare the AI-generated insights with the findings from the
                                     literature review and discuss the key similarities, differences, and
                                     possible reasons for the observed variations. Students must also
                                     disclose the AI tool and prompts used.

 Group Assignment         Build Your Gen Z Smart Equity Portfolio
 Details
 Assignment description   Students will work in groups as a young investment advisory team managing
                          a hypothetical investment fund for Gen Z investors. The assignment integrates
                          equity valuation, portfolio construction, portfolio performance evaluation, and
                          investment communication using contemporary portfolio management
                          techniques.
                          Phase 1: Equity Valuation and Analysis
                          Select 3–5 listed Indian equity stocks from different sectors. Analyze the
                          factors influencing their prices and estimate their intrinsic values using
                          appropriate valuation techniques such as P/E Ratio, P/B Ratio, P/S Ratio,
                          and Dividend Valuation Models. Compare intrinsic values with market
                          prices, forecast future price movements, and identify undervalued or
                          overvalued stocks.
                          Deliverable: A valuation summary table and analytical interpretation
                          Phase 2: Portfolio Construction
                          Construct an equity portfolio for a hypothetical investment amount
                          (₹10,00,000). Justify the allocation based on the risk-return trade-off and
                          apply any two portfolio management concepts such as Modern Portfolio
                          Theory (MPT), CAPM, or Sharpe's Single Index Model.
                          Deliverable: Portfolio allocation chart and risk-return analysis
                          Phase 3: Portfolio Evaluation and Gen Z Pitch
                          Evaluate the portfolio using Sharpe Ratio, Treynor Ratio, and Jensen's
                          Alpha. Present the investment strategy through a 5-minute Gen Z-style
                          digital pitch (Reel, Canva presentation, or Digital Poster) highlighting
                          portfolio suitability, expected returns, risks, and long-term investment
                          strategy.
                          Deliverable: Written Report (2500–3000 words), Creative Digital Pitch
                          Presentation

                          Mock Stock Challenge
                          Each group will participate in two Mock Stock Market trading sessions, where
                          portfolio decisions and investment strategies will be tested in a simulated
                          market environment. Performance in the simulation, investment rationale, and
                          decision-making process will contribute 25% of the overall assignment marks.
