# Management Accounting: Map of Content

## What this is

Entry point for Management Accounting (BBA305-6, Christ University BBA Semester VI, 3 credits, 45 hours). Each unit has a node map and a set of recall notes, ending in a "Unit N Exam Answers" node. Syllabus text: `docs/course-plans/management-accounting.txt`.

## Exam shape

CIA 70% (CIA1 assignment 20; CIA2 mid-semester 50 scaled to 25; CIA3 assignment 20) and ESE 30% (50 marks scaled to 30), plus attendance 5. ESE pattern: Section A 3 questions with internal choice of 5 marks each (15); Section B 2 questions with internal choice of 10 marks each (20); Section C one compulsory 15-mark case study. Essential reading: Arora, *Cost and Management Accounting* (Himalaya, 2019).

## ESE node maps

- [[Management Accounting Unit 1 - Fundamentals MOC (Node Map)]] (6 hrs): FM-1 to FM-4. Meaning and scope, financial vs cost vs management accounting, role and tools, ethics.
- [[Management Accounting Unit 2 - Financial Statement Analysis MOC (Node Map)]] (10 hrs): FS-1 to FS-6. Cash flow statement (rules and full problem), comparative and common-size, ratios, interpretation and limitations.
- [[Management Accounting Unit 3 - Budgeting MOC (Node Map)]] (9 hrs): BU-1 to BU-6. Budgeting concepts, flexible budgets, functional and master budgets, cash budget, ZBB, performance budgeting, behaviour.
- [[Management Accounting Unit 4 - Performance Measurement MOC (Node Map)]] (10 hrs): SC-1 to SC-7. Material, labour and overhead variances, integrated problem, ROI and RI, balanced scorecard and KPIs.
- [[Management Accounting Unit 5 - Sustainability and Responsibility MOC (Node Map)]] (10 hrs): SR-1 to SR-5. Sustainability accounting and ESG, environmental and social costs, responsibility centres, strategy and ethics.

## Hardest topics (extra worked numericals)

1. Variance analysis: SC-1 (material price, usage, mix, yield), SC-2 (labour rate, efficiency, idle, mix, yield), SC-3 (overhead), SC-4 (all together with a reconciliation).
2. Cash flow statement, indirect method: FS-1 (rules) and FS-2 (full problem with sale of fixed assets, tax, proposed dividend, hidden depreciation).
3. Flexible and cash budgets: BU-2 (semi-variable split) and BU-4 (collection and payment lags).

## Recall prompts

- Q: Variance check: which two sums must always hold for materials?
- Q: In a cash flow statement, what is the dividend paid during the year?
- Q: Why can a cash budget show a shortfall in a profitable month?
- Q: A division's ROI falls when it takes a project. Should it reject it?
- Q: What does a carbon-adjusted residual income change in a division ranking?
