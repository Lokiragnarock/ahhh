# Corporate Valuation: Map of Content

## What this is
Entry point for Corporate Valuation (BBA304F-6, Christ University BBA Semester VI, 3 credits, 45 hours). Five units, 26 recall nodes, each unit ending with an exam-answers node and a model 15-mark case.

## Exam pattern (from the syllabus)
ESE is 50 marks: Section A 3 × 5 with internal choice, Section B 2 questions (printed as "5 marks each = 20 marks", which does not add up, so **2 × 10 is assumed**), Section C a 15-mark case study. The ESE carries 30 of the 100 final marks. Confirm the Section B split with the faculty.

## Source status
No faculty slides or class notes exist yet. Every note is labelled **(syllabus)** or **(textbook)**; Damodaran's *Investment Valuation* is the essential reading and Koller et al. the main reference. ## Node maps
- [[Corporate Valuation Unit 1 - Framework MOC (Node Map)]]: VF-1 to VF-5, 9 hrs. Value concepts, contexts, the EV to equity bridge, process and bias.
- [[Corporate Valuation Unit 2 - Enterprise DCF MOC (Node Map)]]: FF-1 to FF-6, 10 hrs. FCFF, WACC, forecasting, terminal value by two methods, EV interpretation.
- [[Corporate Valuation Unit 3 - Equity DCF MOC (Node Map)]]: EQ-1 to EQ-5, 9 hrs. FCFE, Gordon, multistage DDM, equity versus firm valuation.

## Study order
VF-1 to VF-4 first (the bridge in VF-3 is used everywhere), then FF-1 to FF-5, EQ-1 to EQ-4, AV-1 to AV-4, AD-1 to AD-4. Read each unit's exam-answers node last and redo its case without looking.
