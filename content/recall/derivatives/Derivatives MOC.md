# Derivatives: Map of Content

## What this is
Entry point for Derivatives (BBA301F-6, BBA Semester VI). The notes are built from the syllabus and from Hull (essential reading), with Indian market context (NSE, lot sizes, SPAN margins). **No faculty slides were available when these were written.** If class notes define a formula or step order differently, write the class version in the exam and treat these notes as the textbook route. Update this page when slides arrive.

## ESE pattern (from the syllabus)
50 marks: Section A three 5-mark questions with internal choice (15), Section B two 10-mark questions with internal choice (20), Section C one 15-mark case study. CIA is 70% (assignments 20 + 20, mid-semester 50 scaled to 25, attendance 5) and the ESE is 30%. Hours: Unit 1 6, Unit 2 7, Unit 3 12, Unit 4 10, Unit 5 10 (45 in total).

## Recall prompts
- Q: A margin table shows balance below maintenance. What do you deposit and why?
- Q: Futures price above the cost-of-carry price. Which trade and what is the profit per share?
- Q: Portfolio ₹5 crore, beta 1.2, index futures contract worth ₹11,00,000. How many contracts, long or short?
- Q: Which two breakevens does a long straddle have, and what is the maximum loss?
- Q: Put-call parity fails with the put too cheap. Which four legs do you trade?
- Q: What do N(d₁) and N(d₂) mean?
