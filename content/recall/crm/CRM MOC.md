# Customer Relationship Management (CRM) — Map of Content

## What this is
Entry point for the CRM ESE notes. There is no course plan PDF, so the unit titles come from the student's note folders and the ESE pattern is assumed to match Bond Market: 50 marks, Section A 3 × 5, Section B 2 × 10, Section C one compulsory 15-mark case. Each unit has a node map, topic nodes and a final exam answers node. Slides are the faculty's word; where student notes differ, the slides are followed and the difference is stated in the node.
