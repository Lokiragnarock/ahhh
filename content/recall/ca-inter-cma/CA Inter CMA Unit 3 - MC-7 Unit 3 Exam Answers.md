## What to remember

- Abnormal loss value = units × (cost − normal scrap) ÷ (input − normal loss units).
- Average method adds opening WIP cost; FIFO keeps it separate.
- Co-products come from different materials or processes (mustard and sunflower oil).
- Hospital composite unit: patient-day. Depreciation on time basis: fixed.

## Flashcards
Q: 100 units, cost ₹160, normal loss 10% sold at ₹0.25, output 80. Abnormal loss value?
A: ₹17.50.

Q: 200 units in, normal loss 20, output 150. Abnormal loss or gain?
A: Abnormal loss of 30 units.

Q: Under the average method, what happens to opening WIP cost?
A: It is added to the current period's costs.

Q: Batch costing is a type of which method?
A: Job costing.

Q: Give an example of co-products.
A: Mustard oil and sunflower oil in an oil processing company.

Q: Composite cost unit for a hospital?
A: Patient-day.

Q: Under NRV, how are selling and distribution costs treated?
A: Deducted from sales value.

Q: Which costing method is not appropriate for educational institutes?
A: Process costing.
