# CC-2: Labour Variances

Covers: labour cost, rate, efficiency, idle time, mix (gang) and yield (sub-efficiency) variances, their responsibilities and checks, with ICAI Illustrations 5-8. **(ICAI SM ch. 13, para 7.2)** Background: [[Management Accounting Unit 4 - SC-2 Labour Variances]].

## Where this fits in the exam

Labour variances with idle time and a gang mix (Ill. 7-8 type) are a regular 5-10 mark question; the paid vs worked hours distinction is where marks are lost.

## Formulas (ICAI)

| Variance | Formula | Hours used |
|---|---|---|
| **Cost (LCV)** | (SH × SR) − (AH paid × AR) | Paid |
| **Rate (LRV)** | AH paid × (SR − AR) | **Paid** |
| **Efficiency (LEV)** | SR × (SH − AH worked) | **Worked** |
| **Idle time** | SR × (AH paid − AH worked) = SR × idle hours (always adverse) | |
| **Mix (gang)** | SR × (RSH − AH worked) | Worked |
| **Yield (sub-efficiency)** | SR × (SH − RSH) | |

SH = standard hours for actual output; RSH = actual hours worked in standard proportion.

**Checks:** LCV = LRV + idle time + LEV (or LRV + LEV if no idle time); LEV = mix + yield.

**Responsibility:** rate → largely external, but the personnel manager negotiates rates; efficiency → production manager or foreman (for controllable causes).

## ICAI Illustration 5

Standard 1,000 h at ₹50; actual 900 h, wages ₹36,000 (₹40). LRV = 900 × 10 = **₹9,000 F**; LEV = 50 × 100 = **₹5,000 F**; LCV = 50,000 − 36,000 = **₹14,000 F**.

## ICAI Illustration 6 (idle time and mixed rates)

Standard output 25 units per hour for 100 workers (so 4 man-hours per unit), rate ₹6. 42-hour week, output 1,040 units; 5% of paid time lost (abnormal). Actual rates: 10 workers ₹6.20, 30 at ₹6, 60 at ₹5.70.

1. SH = 1,040 × 4 = 4,160 h; standard cost ₹24,960.
2. Hours paid 4,200 (420 / 1,260 / 2,520); idle 5% = 210; worked 3,990. Actual cost ₹24,528 (2,604 + 7,560 + 14,364).

| Variance | ₹ |
|---|---|
| Rate: (6 − 6.20) × 420 = 84 A; 0; (6 − 5.70) × 2,520 = 756 F | **672 F** |
| Efficiency: (4,160 − 3,990) × 6 | **1,020 F** |
| Idle time: 210 × 6 | **1,260 A** |
| **Cost** (24,960 − 24,528) | **432 F** ✓ |

## ICAI Illustration 7 (three grades, idle time, mix and yield)

Standard per unit: skilled 2 h at ₹6, semi-skilled 3 h at ₹4, unskilled 5 h at ₹3. Output 10,000 units. Actual paid: skilled 18,000 h at ₹7, semi 33,000 at ₹3.50, unskilled 58,000 at ₹4 (₹4,73,500). Idle (abnormal): 500 / 700 / 800, so worked 17,500 / 32,300 / 57,200 (1,07,000).

SH: 20,000 / 30,000 / 50,000 (1,00,000; standard cost ₹3,90,000). RSH = 1,07,000 in 2:3:5 = 21,400 / 32,100 / 53,500.

| Variance | Skilled | Semi | Unskilled | Total |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Cost | 6,000 A | 4,500 F | 82,000 A | **83,500 A** |
| Rate (on paid hours) | 18,000 A | 16,500 F | 58,000 A | **59,500 A** |
| Efficiency (on worked hours) | 15,000 F | 9,200 A | 21,600 A | **15,800 A** |
| Idle time | 3,000 A | 2,800 A | 2,400 A | **8,200 A** |
| Mix (RSH − AH worked) × SR | 23,400 F | 800 A | 11,100 A | **11,500 F** |
| Yield (SH − RSH) × SR | 8,400 A | 8,400 A | 10,500 A | **27,300 A** |

Checks: 59,500 A + 15,800 A + 8,200 A = 83,500 A ✓; 11,500 F + 27,300 A = 15,800 A ✓.

**Part (b):** if 5,000 skilled hours were paid at ₹5.50 and 13,000 at ₹7: skilled rate variance = 5,000 × 0.50 F − 13,000 × 1 A = 10,500 A; total rate variance **₹52,000 A**.

## ICAI Illustration 8 (gang composition)

Standard gang: 32 skilled (₹3), 12 semi (₹2), 6 unskilled (₹1). Actual: 28 (₹4), 18 (₹3), 4 (₹2). 40-hour week; output = 1,800 standard hours.

- SH (1,800 in the standard 1,280 : 480 : 240 ratio): 1,152 / 432 / 216.
- AH: 1,120 / 720 / 160 (2,000). RSH (2,000 in standard ratio): 1,280 / 480 / 240.

| | SH × SR | AH × SR | AH × AR | RSH × SR |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Total | 4,536 | 4,960 | 6,960 | 5,040 |

- LCV = 4,536 − 6,960 = **₹2,424 A**
- LRV = 4,960 − 6,960 = **₹2,000 A**
- LEV = 4,536 − 4,960 = **₹424 A**
- Mix = 5,040 − 4,960 = **₹80 F**
- Yield = 4,536 − 5,040 = **₹504 A**

The four-column table (SH×SR, AH×SR, AH×AR, RSH×SR) gives every variance by subtraction: a fast exam layout.

## Traps

- Rate variance on hours **worked**. ICAI: on hours **paid**.
- Efficiency on hours **paid**. ICAI: on hours **worked** (idle time separated).
- Idle time is always adverse and valued at **standard** rate.
- RSH = **actual hours worked** in standard proportion (not paid hours).

## What to remember

- LCV = LRV (paid) + idle (paid − worked) + LEV (worked); LEV = mix + yield.
- Ill. 6: rate 672 F, efficiency 1,020 F, idle 1,260 A, cost 432 F.
- Ill. 7: cost 83,500 A = rate 59,500 A + efficiency 15,800 A + idle 8,200 A; mix 11,500 F, yield 27,300 A.
- Ill. 8: cost 2,424 A; rate 2,000 A; efficiency 424 A (mix 80 F, yield 504 A).

## Flashcards
Q: Labour rate variance formula, and on which hours?
A: AH paid × (SR − AR).

Q: Labour efficiency variance formula, and on which hours?
A: SR × (SH − AH worked).

Q: Idle time variance formula?
A: SR × (hours paid − hours worked); always adverse.

Q: Labour mix (gang) variance formula?
A: SR × (RSH − AH worked).

Q: Labour yield variance formula?
A: SR × (SH − RSH).

Q: Full labour check?
A: LCV = LRV + idle time variance + LEV; LEV = mix + yield.

Q: ICAI Ill. 6: idle time variance?
A: ₹1,260 A (210 hours × ₹6).

Q: ICAI Ill. 8: labour mix and yield variances?
A: Mix ₹80 F; yield ₹504 A.

Q: Who is usually responsible for labour efficiency variance?
A: The production manager or foreman (for controllable causes).
