# OV-6: Unit 1 Exam Answers

Covers: how Unit 1 is examined, ICAI's own end-of-chapter MCQs with ICAI's keys, layouts for the ICAI theory questions, and the reverse (working-backwards) cost sheet from ICAI Practical Problem 1 of Chapter 6. Every question here is ICAI's. **(ICAI SM ch. 1 and 6, Test Your Knowledge)**

## How Unit 1 is examined

- **Part I (MCQs, 2 marks each):** definitions, cost units, classification of single items into cost-sheet heads. Fast marks if the vocabulary is sharp.
- **Part II (descriptive):** a 4-5 mark theory part (objectives, installation factors, cost centres, methods with cost units), or a 5-10 mark cost sheet.
- Theory answers: use ICAI's headings as your points, one line of explanation each. Command words: DESCRIBE, DISCUSS, STATE.

## Reverse cost sheet (ICAI Ch. 6 Practical Problem 1)

Adarsh Manufacturing, April. Direct labour ₹17,500 = 175% of works overheads. Cost of goods sold (excluding admin) ₹56,000. Stocks, 1 April / 30 April: raw materials 8,000 / 10,600; WIP 10,500 / 14,500; finished goods 17,600 / 19,000. Selling ₹3,500; general admin ₹2,500; sales ₹75,000. Find materials purchased and profit.

**Work backwards, reversing every sign:**

| | ₹ |
|---|---|
| Cost of goods sold | 56,000 |
| + Closing FG − opening FG (19,000 − 17,600) | 1,400 |
| **Cost of production** | 57,400 |
| + Closing WIP − opening WIP (14,500 − 10,500) | 4,000 |
| **Works cost** | 61,400 |
| − Works overheads (17,500 × 100 ÷ 175) | (10,000) |
| **Prime cost** | 51,400 |
| − Direct labour | (17,500) |
| **Material consumed** | 33,900 |
| + Closing RM − opening RM (10,600 − 8,000) | 2,600 |
| **Materials purchased** | **36,500** |

Forward: cost of goods sold 56,000 + admin 2,500 + selling 3,500 = cost of sales 62,000; profit = 75,000 − 62,000 = **₹13,000**.

**Rule:** going backwards, closing stock is **added** and opening stock **subtracted**, the reverse of the forward sheet.

## Traps

- "Lowest cost under best conditions" is the classic wrong option. ICAI says **existing** conditions.
- A special frame or pattern bought for one order is a **direct expense**, not direct material.
- Royalty on production is a direct expense; royalty on sales would be a selling cost.

## What to remember

- Ch. 1 keys: b, c, b, b, a, b, b, d, c, b. Ch. 6 keys: a, a, b, b, a, b, a, a, a, c.
- Reverse cost sheet: add closing, subtract opening; works overhead = labour × 100 ÷ 175.
- Adarsh: purchases ₹36,500; profit ₹13,000.

## Flashcards
Q: Railway ticket counter: what kind of responsibility centre?
A: Revenue centre.

Q: Taxi with a minimum fare plus a per-km rate: cost behaviour?
A: Semi-variable.

Q: Special frame purchased for a particular order: which element?
A: Direct expenses.

Q: Salary of staff in a lab testing products for standards compliance?
A: Quality control cost.

Q: Reverse cost sheet: going from cost of goods sold to cost of production, what do you do with finished goods stock?
A: Add closing stock and subtract opening stock.

Q: Direct labour ₹17,500 is 175% of works overheads. Works overheads?
A: ₹10,000.

Q: Method and cost unit for a hospital?
A: Operating (service) costing; patient day.

Q: Method and cost unit for brick-works?
A: Single or output costing; per 1,000 bricks.
