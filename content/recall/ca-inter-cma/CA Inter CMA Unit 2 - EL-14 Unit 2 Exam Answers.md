# EL-14: Unit 2 Exam Answers

Covers: how Unit 2 (35-40% of the paper) is examined, ICAI's own end-of-chapter MCQs with ICAI's keys, and the layouts for the recurring Part II questions. Every question here is ICAI's. **(ICAI SM chs. 2, 3, 4, 5, 7, Test Your Knowledge)**

## How Unit 2 is examined

Unit 2 is the heaviest unit, so expect at least one full 14-mark Part II question from it and several MCQs. The standard question types:

| Type | Node | Layout |
|---|---|---|
| Rate per kg after shortages, stores ledger entry price | EL-1 | Effective quantity table, then cost table, then rate |
| Stores ledger (FIFO or weighted average) with returns and losses | EL-2 | Receipts / issues / balance columns; returns to stores at issue rate; normal loss lifts the rate, abnormal loss to Costing P&L |
| EOQ, stock levels, safety stock | EL-3 | Formula, substitution, answer; check ordering cost = carrying cost |
| ABC plan, inventory turnover | EL-4 | Value, rank, class; turnover = consumption ÷ average stock |
| Effective hourly rate, overtime premium treatment | EL-5 | Annual cost ÷ effective hours |
| Halsey vs Rowan, labour turnover, profit foregone | EL-6 | Formulas; equations when working back |
| Primary + secondary distribution + machine hour rate | EL-8 to EL-10 | Overhead distribution summary, then the named secondary method, then MHR |
| Under-absorption and supplementary rate | EL-10 | Actual − absorbed; split genuine vs abnormal; spread genuine over FG, WIP, cost of sales |
| Traditional vs ABC unit cost | EL-11 | Rates per driver, then per-unit costs side by side |
| Cost ledger journal entries, control accounts | EL-12 | Journal, ledger accounts, trial balance with what each balance means |
| Reconciliation statement | EL-13 | Start from one profit; add or subtract each item |

**Q9 logic:** the cost books charged ₹600 less admin than actual, so the financial loss is bigger: 5,600 + 600 = ₹6,200.

## Theory questions ICAI sets (and where the answer is)

- Treatment of normal and abnormal losses of material (ch. 2, para 10): table of waste, scrap, spoilage, defectives, obsolescence.
- Treatment of idle time and overtime (ch. 3, paras 5-6): normal vs abnormal; four overtime causes and treatments.
- Classification of overheads; treatment of special items (ch. 4, paras 2, 11).
- Terms used in ABC; ABC vs traditional costing (ch. 5, paras 3, 5).
- Prerequisites of integrated accounts (ch. 7, para 3.2): extent of integration; coding; routine for accruals; co-ordination.

## What to remember

- Simple average gives absurd results when prices swing; weighted average smooths them.
- Rowan pays more below 50% time saved.
- Normal capacity basis keeps under/over absorption smallest.
- From costing loss, add under-absorbed overhead to get the (bigger) financial loss.

## Flashcards
Q: Which pricing method gives absurd results when prices fluctuate widely?
A: Simple average price.

Q: Under which method do issue costs lag behind current values?
A: FIFO.

Q: Rowan wages: standard 60 h, ₹0.30 per hour, taken 48 h?
A: ₹17.28.

Q: Which capacity basis keeps under/over absorption to a minimum?
A: Normal capacity.

Q: Directors' remuneration is which overhead?
A: Administration overhead.

Q: Costing loss ₹5,600, admin under-absorbed ₹600. Financial loss?
A: ₹6,200.

Q: Which item is included in cost accounts: notional rent, donations, transfer to reserve or rent receivable?
A: Notional rent.
