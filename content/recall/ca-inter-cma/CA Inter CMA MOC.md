# CA Inter CMA: Map of Content

## What this is

Entry point for ICAI CA Intermediate **Paper 4: Cost and Management Accounting** (Group II). Notes are written from ICAI's own study material (July 2026 edition, relevant for May 2027 onwards); every worked example is an ICAI illustration, practical problem or past-paper question, cited by number. Plan: `docs/ca-plan.md`.

## Exam shape

3 hours, 100 marks. Part I: 30 marks of MCQs (2 marks each, case scenarios of five plus standalone MCQs, no negative marking). Part II: 70 marks, six questions, Question 1 compulsory and any 4 of the other 5 (14 marks each); working notes form part of the answer. Pass: 40% in each paper and 50% aggregate across Group II.

- [[CA Inter CMA Unit 1 - Overview MOC (Node Map)]] (10-15%): OV-1 to OV-6. Objectives, classification, cost behaviour, methods, cost sheet. ICAI ch. 1, 6.
- [[CA Inter CMA Unit 2 - Ascertainment of Cost MOC (Node Map)]] (35-40%): EL-1 to EL-14. Material, employee cost, direct expenses, overheads, ABC, cost accounting systems, reconciliation. ICAI ch. 2-5, 7.
- [[CA Inter CMA Unit 3 - Methods of Costing MOC (Node Map)]] (20-25%): MC-1 to MC-7. Unit and batch, job, process, joint and by-products, service costing. ICAI ch. 8-12.
- [[CA Inter CMA Unit 4 - Cost Control and Analysis MOC (Node Map)]] (25-30%): CC-1 to CC-11. Standard costing, marginal costing, budgets. ICAI ch. 13-15.

## Priority order if short of time

1. Unit 2 (35-40%): overheads (EL-8 to EL-10), material (EL-2 to EL-4), reconciliation (EL-13).
2. Unit 4 (25-30%): standard costing (CC-1 to CC-4), marginal costing (CC-5 to CC-7).
3. Unit 3 (20-25%): process costing (MC-3, MC-4), service costing (MC-6).
4. Unit 1 (10-15%): cost sheet (OV-5) and MCQ vocabulary.
