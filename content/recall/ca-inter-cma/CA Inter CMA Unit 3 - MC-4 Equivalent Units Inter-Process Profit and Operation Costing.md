# MC-4: Equivalent Units, WIP Valuation, Inter-Process Profit and Operation Costing

Covers: valuation of work-in-process; equivalent units and the standard statement; the five steps of a production cost report; FIFO vs weighted average with ICAI Illustrations 4-5; inter-process profit with Illustration 6; and operation (hybrid) costing. **(ICAI SM ch. 10, paras 4-8)**

## Where this fits in the exam

Equivalent production with opening and closing WIP, normal loss and abnormal loss is a top 14-mark question; FIFO vs average is often asked side by side. Inter-process profit appears every few attempts and is easy marks if the layout is known.

## Equivalent units

Partly finished units are converted into equivalent finished units for each cost element:

**Equivalent units = units in process × percentage of work completed** (per element: material, labour, overhead). 200 units 25% complete = 50 equivalent units.

Rules for the statement:
- **Normal loss:** no equivalent units.
- **Abnormal loss:** at its degree of completion (100% if lost at the end).
- **Abnormal gain:** always **100%** for all elements; shown as a deduction.
- **Opening WIP:** separate line only under **FIFO** (the work still to do).
- **Finished output:** FIFO = units completed **less** opening WIP; average = all units completed.

## Five steps (production cost report)

1. Physical flow of units (inputs vs outputs).
2. Equivalent units for each cost element (e.g. 1,000 closing units, material added at start, 80% done for conversion: material 1,000, labour and overhead 800).
3. Total cost of each element.
4. Cost per equivalent unit = cost ÷ equivalent units.
5. Assign cost to units completed, closing WIP, abnormal loss.

## FIFO vs weighted average

| | FIFO | Weighted average |
|---|---|---|
| Opening WIP | Completed first; only the **remaining** work is costed this period | Merged with current units as if started this period |
| Cost pool | Current period cost only | Opening WIP cost + current cost |
| Closing WIP | At current cost | At average cost |
| Cost of opening WIP units | Its b/f cost + cost to complete | Not separated |

## ICAI Illustration 4 (FIFO)

Opening WIP 1,000 units (60% complete), cost ₹1,10,000; introduced 10,000 units, cost ₹19,30,000; transferred 9,000; closing WIP 800 (75%); normal loss 10% of total input (opening + introduced) = 1,100 units, scrap ₹10, 100% complete.

| Output | Units | % | EU |
|---|---|---|---|
| Opening WIP completed | 1,000 | 40 | 400 |
| Fresh units completed | 8,000 | 100 | 8,000 |
| Normal loss | 1,100 | | |
| Closing WIP | 800 | 75 | 600 |
| Abnormal loss (balancing: 11,000 − 9,000 − 1,100 − 800) | 100 | 100 | 100 |
| **Total** | **11,000** | | **9,100** |

Cost = 19,30,000 − 11,000 scrap = ₹19,19,000; **₹210.88 per EU**.

| Evaluation | ₹ |
|---|---|
| Opening WIP: 400 × 210.88 = 84,352 + b/f 1,10,000 | 1,94,352 |
| Fresh completed: 8,000 × 210.88 | 16,87,040 |
| Abnormal loss: 100 × 210.88 | 21,088 |
| Closing WIP: 600 × 210.88 | 1,26,528 |

## ICAI Illustration 5 (same data, weighted average)

EU = completed 9,000 + closing WIP 600 + abnormal loss 100 = **9,700**. Cost = 1,10,000 + 19,30,000 − 11,000 = ₹20,29,000; **₹209.18 per EU**. Completed 9,000 × 209.18 = ₹18,82,620; abnormal loss ₹20,918; closing WIP ₹1,25,508.

## Inter-process profit

When processes are profit centres, output moves to the next process at market value or cost plus a profit; the difference is **inter-process profit**. Stocks then contain unrealised profit, which must be removed.

**Advantages:** compares the cost of output with its market price at each stage; each process stands on its own profitability. **Disadvantages:** complicated; shows profits not yet realised (stock unsold).

### ICAI Illustration 6

Process I → II at 25% profit on **transfer price**; II → finished stock at 20% on transfer price. Process stocks at prime cost; finished stock at the price received from II. Sales ₹1,40,000.

**Process I:** opening 7,500 + materials 15,000 + wages 11,200 = prime 33,700; overheads 10,500 → 44,200; less closing 3,700 = 40,500; transfer price = 40,500 ÷ 75% = **₹54,000** (profit ₹13,500).

**Process II:** opening 9,000 (cost 7,500, profit 1,500) + 54,000 (40,500 + 13,500) + materials 15,750 + wages 11,250 = prime 90,000 (cost 75,000, profit 15,000); overheads 4,500 → 94,500; closing stock 4,500 contains profit in proportion 15,000 ÷ 90,000, so cost 3,750, profit 750; transfer = (94,500 − 4,500) ÷ 80% = **₹1,12,500** (profit ₹22,500; cost element 75,750, profit element 36,750).

**Finished stock:** opening 22,500 (cost 14,250, profit 8,250) + 1,12,500; closing 11,250 → cost element 11,250 × 75,750 ÷ 1,12,500 = 7,575, profit 3,675; sales 1,40,000; profit at this stage **₹16,250**.

| Profit realised | ₹ |
|---|---|
| Process I | 13,500 |
| Process II | 22,500 |
| Finished stock | 16,250 |
| Total booked | 52,250 |

The **cost** column shows the true picture: cost of goods sold 82,425 against sales 1,40,000 gives a realised profit of ₹57,575. Check: booked 52,250 + unrealised profit in opening stocks released (1,500 + 8,250 = 9,750) − unrealised profit in closing stocks (750 + 3,675 = 4,425) = 57,575 ✓.

**Method:** carry three columns (total, cost, profit) in every account; split closing stocks in the ratio of cost to total in that account; transfer price = cost to transfer ÷ (1 − margin on price).

## Operation costing (hybrid)

Used when variants of a product use **different materials but similar conversion**. Materials are accumulated by job or batch; **conversion costs** (labour and overhead) by department, applied at a **pre-determined rate** based on budgeted conversion cost (process-style). Examples: ready-made garments, shoes, jewellery (a Deluxe and a Regular product with different materials, same processing).

## Traps

- Under FIFO, counting opening WIP at 100%. Only the **remaining** % (here 40%).
- Under average, forgetting to add opening WIP **cost** to the pool.
- Abnormal gain at the WIP completion %. It is always 100%.
- Margin on transfer price vs on cost: 25% on price = cost ÷ 0.75.

## What to remember

- EU = units × % complete; normal loss none; abnormal gain 100%.
- Ill. 4 FIFO: 9,100 EU, ₹210.88; opening WIP finished ₹1,94,352. Ill. 5 average: 9,700 EU, ₹209.18.
- Inter-process profit: three columns; transfer = cost ÷ (1 − margin); unrealised profit removed from closing stocks.
- Operation costing: materials by batch, conversion by department.

## Flashcards
Q: Formula for equivalent units?
A: Units in process × percentage of work completed (for each cost element).

Q: Equivalent units for normal loss?
A: None.

Q: Degree of completion for abnormal gain?
A: Always 100% for all elements.

Q: Under FIFO, how is opening WIP 60% complete treated?
A: Only the remaining 40% of work is costed in the current period; its brought-forward cost is added separately.

Q: Main difference between FIFO and weighted average in process costing?
A: FIFO costs only the work done this period and values closing WIP at current cost; average merges opening WIP cost and units with the current period.

Q: ICAI Ill. 4: cost per equivalent unit under FIFO?
A: ₹210.88 (₹19,19,000 ÷ 9,100).

Q: ICAI Ill. 5: cost per equivalent unit under average?
A: ₹209.18 (₹20,29,000 ÷ 9,700).

Q: Transfer at 25% profit on transfer price; cost to transfer ₹40,500. Transfer price?
A: ₹54,000.

Q: What is operation costing?
A: A hybrid: materials costed by job or batch, conversion costs by department at a pre-determined rate; for variants with different materials but similar processing.
