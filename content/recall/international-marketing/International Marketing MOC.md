## Recall prompts
- Q: Name the three entry-mode families from least to most commitment.
  (Exporting, then contractual modes such as licensing and franchising, then FDI.)
- Q: What is price escalation?
  (The rise in an export's shelf price over its ex-works price as freight, insurance, duties and channel margins stack up.)
- Q: Driving vs restraining forces, one example each?
  (Driving: technology, regional economic agreements. Restraining: management myopia, national controls.)
- Q: Porter's five forces?
  (New entrants, substitutes, buyer power, supplier power, rivalry among existing competitors.)
