# Taxation Units 1 & 2 — MCQ Practice Bank

Prep for the **20-question CIA1-B Google Form test** on Units 1 & 2. Covers basic concepts, residential status & incidence, agricultural/exempt income, heads of income, and Income from Salary — the topics your own notes ([[Income Tax]], [[Residential Status]], [[Agriculture Income - Sec 10(1)]], [[incidence of tax]]) sit on.

> **Scope assumption:** Unit 1 = basic concepts + residential status + exempt income; Unit 2 = heads of income (esp. Salary). If your syllabus splits differently, tell me and I'll re-cut.
> **Regime scope:** every question is answered under the **new regime (Sec 115BAC)**.
> **Figure warning:** questions marked **[₹]** depend on the assessment year / Budget. Re-keyed to **AY 2026-27** (per the course plan's reference text and CIA-3 ITR-form year — see [[BBA303-5 - Taxation Laws and Practice Course Plan]]), not AY 2025-26 as originally drafted. Confirm against your professor if this shifts.

Answer key + one-line rationale is at the bottom. Try before you scroll.

## A. Basic concepts & definitions (Unit 1)

**1.** The Income Tax Act, 1961 came into force on:
a) 1 April 1961  b) 1 April 1962  c) 1 April 1965  d) 1 January 1962

**2.** "Assessment Year" under Sec 2(9) means the period of 12 months starting from:
a) 1 January  b) 1 April  c) 1 July  d) any date the assessee chooses

**3.** Income earned in the Previous Year 2024-25 is taxed in the Assessment Year:
a) 2024-25  b) 2025-26  c) 2023-24  d) 2026-27

**4.** Which of the following is NOT included in the definition of "Person" under Sec 2(31)?
a) HUF  b) A local authority  c) A partnership firm  d) A crop of wheat

**5.** "Assessee" under Sec 2(7) includes:
a) only a person who pays tax  b) a person liable to pay tax or any other sum, or against whom proceedings have been taken  c) only companies  d) only individuals earning above the exemption limit

**6.** Gross Total Income minus deductions under Chapter VI-A equals:
a) Total Income  b) Taxable salary  c) Book profit  d) Net agricultural income

**7. [₹]** For an individual under the **new regime**, the basic exemption (nil) slab goes up to:
a) ₹2,50,000  b) ₹3,00,000  c) ₹4,00,000  d) ₹7,00,000

**8.** The concept where the first previous year for a newly set-up business can be shorter than 12 months is because:
a) the AY is always 12 months  b) the PY begins on the date of setting up and ends on the following 31 March  c) the assessee elects it  d) it never happens

## B. Residential status & incidence of tax (Unit 1)

**9.** Residential status of an assessee is determined under:
a) Sec 5  b) Sec 6  c) Sec 9  d) Sec 10

**10.** An individual is a "resident" if they are in India for at least:
a) 182 days in the PY, OR 60 days in the PY + 365 days in the 4 preceding years  b) 90 days only  c) 730 days in 7 years only  d) 182 days in the AY

**11.** To qualify as "Resident and Ordinarily Resident" (ROR), an individual must additionally be:
a) resident in 2 of the last 10 years AND in India ≥ 730 days in the last 7 years  b) resident for 5 continuous years  c) a citizen of India  d) present for 182 days in each of 3 years

**12.** Which type of assessee is taxed on their global income (income earned anywhere in the world)?
a) Non-Resident  b) Resident and Ordinarily Resident  c) Resident but Not Ordinarily Resident  d) All assessees equally

**13.** Income that accrues and is received outside India is taxable for:
a) NR only  b) RNOR only  c) ROR only  d) everyone

**14.** Income deemed to accrue or arise in India is taxable:
a) only for residents  b) for all assessees regardless of residential status  c) only for citizens  d) never

**15.** Scope of total income based on residential status is governed by:
a) Sec 6  b) Sec 5  c) Sec 14  d) Sec 80C

## C. Agricultural & exempt income (Unit 1)

**16.** Agricultural income is exempt under:
a) Sec 10(1)  b) Sec 10(2)  c) Sec 11  d) Sec 80G

**17.** Agricultural income is exempt because, under the Constitution, tax on it is a subject of:
a) the Union  b) the States  c) concurrent list  d) local bodies

**18.** "Partial integration" of agricultural income means:
a) agricultural income is taxed directly  b) agricultural income is added only to determine the tax rate on non-agricultural income  c) 50% of it is taxed  d) it is taxed in the next year

**19.** Which of these is NOT agricultural income?
a) Rent from agricultural land  b) Income from sale of standing crop  c) Income from a poultry farm  d) Income from growing and selling tea (agricultural portion)

**20.** Income from growing AND manufacturing tea in India is treated as:
a) 100% agricultural  b) 100% business  c) 60% agricultural, 40% business  d) 40% agricultural, 60% business

**21.** Which of the following is an exempt income under Sec 10?
a) Salary  b) Share of profit from a partnership firm received by a partner  c) Interest on fixed deposit  d) Rent from house property

## D. Heads of income (Unit 2)

**22.** The heads of income are enumerated in:
a) Sec 10  b) Sec 14  c) Sec 2  d) Sec 80

**23.** How many heads of income are there under the Income Tax Act?
a) 3  b) 4  c) 5  d) 6

**24.** Interest earned on a savings bank account is taxable under:
a) Income from Salary  b) Income from House Property  c) Profits & Gains of Business or Profession  d) Income from Other Sources

**25.** Profit on sale of a residential house held for 3 years is taxed under:
a) Income from House Property  b) Capital Gains  c) Other Sources  d) Business income

**26.** Rental income from letting out a house property is taxed under:
a) Capital Gains  b) Income from House Property  c) Other Sources  d) Salary

## E. Income from Salary (Unit 2)

**27.** Income from Salary is charged under:
a) Sec 15  b) Sec 22  c) Sec 28  d) Sec 45

**28.** The relationship essential for income to be taxed as "salary" is:
a) principal–agent  b) employer–employee  c) partner–firm  d) lender–borrower

**29. [₹]** Standard deduction available to a salaried employee under Sec 16(ia) under the **new regime** for AY 2026-27 is:
a) ₹40,000  b) ₹50,000  c) ₹75,000  d) not available

**30.** Which of the following is a fully taxable allowance?
a) Dearness Allowance  b) Children Education Allowance (up to limit)  c) HRA (fully, in all cases)  d) fully exempt travel

**31.** A "perquisite" under Sec 17(2) refers to:
a) salary in cash only  b) any benefit or amenity granted by the employer in addition to salary  c) a loan from a bank  d) agricultural income

**32.** Which of the following is taxable as salary?
a) Pension received by an employee  b) Gift from a friend  c) Dividend from shares  d) Rent from property

**33.** Entertainment allowance deduction under Sec 16(ii) is available to:
a) all employees  b) only government employees (within limits)  c) only private employees  d) no one

**34. [₹]** Under Sec 87A, a resident individual under the **new regime** (AY 2026-27) gets a full rebate if total income does not exceed:
a) ₹5,00,000  b) ₹7,00,000  c) ₹12,00,000  d) ₹2,50,000

## Answer key + rationale

1. **b** — Act of 1961, effective 1 April 1962.
2. **b** — AY runs 1 April to 31 March.
3. **b** — PY 2024-25 → assessed in AY 2025-26.
4. **d** — a crop is not a "person"; the seven categories are individual, HUF, company, firm, AOP/BOI, local authority, artificial juridical person.
5. **b** — Sec 2(7) is broad: liable to pay tax/any sum, or subject to proceedings.
6. **a** — GTI − Chapter VI-A deductions = Total Income.
7. **c** — ₹4,00,000 nil slab, new regime FY 2025-26/AY 2026-27. **[₹ verify AY]**
8. **b** — PY of a new business starts on the setup date, ends 31 March.
9. **b** — Sec 6 determines residential status.
10. **a** — the two basic conditions of Sec 6(1).
11. **a** — the two additional conditions for ROR.
12. **b** — ROR is taxed on global income.
13. **c** — foreign-accrued, foreign-received income is taxable only for ROR.
14. **b** — deemed-to-accrue-in-India income is taxed for everyone regardless of status.
15. **b** — Sec 5 defines scope of total income; Sec 6 defines status.
16. **a** — Sec 10(1).
17. **b** — agriculture is a State subject, so the Union does not tax agricultural income directly.
18. **b** — partial integration uses agri income only to set the slab rate on non-agri income (applies when agri income > ₹5,000 and non-agri income exceeds the exemption limit).
19. **c** — poultry farming is not agricultural income (no cultivation of land).
20. **c** — Rule 8: tea grown + manufactured = 60% agricultural (exempt), 40% business (taxable).
21. **b** — a partner's share of firm profit is exempt in the partner's hands under Sec 10(2A).
22. **b** — Sec 14 lists the heads.
23. **c** — five heads: Salary, House Property, PGBP, Capital Gains, Other Sources.
24. **d** — savings interest falls under Income from Other Sources.
25. **b** — sale of a capital asset → Capital Gains.
26. **b** — rental income → Income from House Property.
27. **a** — Sec 15 charges salary.
28. **b** — employer–employee relationship is essential.
29. **c** — ₹75,000 standard deduction, new regime, AY 2026-27. **[₹ verify AY]**
30. **a** — DA is fully taxable.
31. **b** — perquisite = benefit/amenity in addition to salary (Sec 17(2)).
32. **a** — pension is taxable as salary (uncommuted) / partly on commutation.
33. **b** — Sec 16(ii) entertainment allowance deduction is for government employees only, within limits.
34. **c** — full 87A rebate up to ₹12,00,000 total income under the new regime, AY 2026-27 (this rose from ₹7,00,000 the prior AY — don't mix them up). **[₹ verify AY]**
