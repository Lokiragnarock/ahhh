## Recall prompts
- Q: What does the triple bottom line add to the usual bottom line?
  (People and Planet alongside Profit. John Elkington coined the term in the 1990s.)
- Q: Green bond vs sustainability-linked bond, in one line each?
  (Green bond: the money must go to green projects. SLB: the money can go anywhere, but the coupon depends on hitting ESG targets.)
- Q: Physical vs transition risk?
  (Physical: damage from the climate itself, such as floods and heat. Transition: losses from the move to a low-carbon economy, such as carbon prices, new rules and stranded assets.)
- Q: Expected loss formula?
  (EL = PD × LGD × EAD. ESG factors can raise any of the three.)
