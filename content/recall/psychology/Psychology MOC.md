# Psychology — Map of Content

Entry point for prathyu's B.Sc. Psychology Semester 1 recall notes: three theory papers, five units each, plus the Practical I skills reference. Distilled from the official syllabus (`14PSU01`, `14PSU02`, `14PSU03`, and the Experimental Psychology Practical I course description).

## 14PSU01 — General Psychology I (prefix: GP1)
- [[GP1 - Unit 1 Introduction and Methods]] — definition/goals of psychology, history (Wundt 1879), schools (structuralism, functionalism, behaviourism, Gestalt, psychoanalysis), modern perspectives, psychology in India, the six research methods, basic vs applied scope
- [[GP1 - Unit 2 Sensation and Perception]] — nervous system divisions, sensation (thresholds, adaptation), all sense modalities, perception (constancies, illusions, depth, ESP), attention (types, determinants)
- [[GP1 - Unit 3 Consciousness and Altered States]] — consciousness, biological rhythms, waking state, sleep stages/function/disorders, dreams and dream theories, hypnosis/drugs/meditation
- [[GP1 - Unit 4 Learning]] — classical conditioning (Pavlov), operant conditioning (Skinner, reinforcement/punishment), cognitive learning (learned helplessness, observational learning, insight learning)
- [[GP1 - Unit 5 Memory and Forgetting]] — encoding/storage/retrieval, information-processing model (sensory/STM/LTM), theories of forgetting, memory and the brain, techniques for improving memory

## 14PSU02 — Life Span Development I (prefix: LSD1)
- [[LSD1 - Unit 1 Introduction to Lifespan Development]] — concept and nature of development, periods, developmental issues (nature/nurture, continuity/discontinuity, stability/change), importance, characteristics of the lifespan approach
- [[LSD1 - Unit 2 Theories of Development and Prenatal Period]] — Erikson's psychosocial stages, Piaget's cognitive stages, genetic foundations (genes/chromosomes), prenatal development (germinal/embryonic/fetal), prenatal care and hazards
- [[LSD1 - Unit 3 Infancy and Its Development]] — birth stages, Apgar assessment, postpartum period, infancy growth patterns, motor development (reflexes, gross/fine motor), sensory development (vision, hearing, touch, smell, taste)
- [[LSD1 - Unit 4 Physical Motor and Cognitive Development]] — early and middle/late childhood physical/motor development and health, Piaget's preoperational and concrete operational stages, information processing, language development across both periods
- [[LSD1 - Unit 5 Socio-Emotional Development]] — infancy attachment and social understanding, early childhood self/emotional/moral/gender development plus family/peers/play/television, middle/late childhood emotional development plus family/peers/schools

## 14PSU03 — Elements of Sociology (prefix: SOC)
- [[SOC - Unit 1 Origin and Development of Sociology]] — definition and meaning, historical origin (industrial/French revolutions, urbanisation), key thinkers (Comte, Spencer, Durkheim, Marx, Weber), nature and scope, relation to psychology
- [[SOC - Unit 2 Primary Concepts]] — society, community, association, institution, socialization (agents, primary/secondary), social groups (primary/secondary, in-group/out-group), status and role
- [[SOC - Unit 3 Family and Marriage]] — family meaning/origin/characteristics/functions, types of family (structure/residence/ancestry), modern family features, marriage meaning/characteristics/functions/forms
- [[SOC - Unit 4 Social Process and Social Control]] — social interaction, the five social processes (cooperation, competition, conflict, accommodation, assimilation), social control (types, agencies, importance)
- [[SOC - Unit 5 Culture and Social Change]] — culture meaning/characteristics/components/functions, cultural change and process, social change meaning/nature/characteristics/factors

## Practical I — skills reference (not a trackable topic)
- [[Prathyu Psychology Semester 1 - Experimental Psychology Practical I Reference]] — professional skills (attention/concentration, learning methods) and the 16-experiment menu (sensory, attention, perceptual, learning) across 4 categories; choose any 8

## How the units are ordered
Each paper's units build on the one before it (GP1-U1 → U2 → U3 → U4 → U5, and similarly for LSD1 and SOC), tracked via `deps` in each note's frontmatter. Exam-focus units are flagged where the topic is conceptually central or historically well-tested: GP1 U2/U4/U5, LSD1 U2/U4, SOC U2/U3.
