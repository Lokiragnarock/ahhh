Official ETS GRE material, free downloads, fetched 2026-11-01. Private use only.

Files
- gre-screen-reader-practice-test-1-quant.docx, gre-screen-reader-practice-test-1-answer-key.docx
  https://www.ets.org/content/dam/ets-org/pdfs/gre/  (listed on
  https://www.ets.org/gre/test-takers/general-test/prepare/test-prep-accessible-formats.html)
  Practice Test 1: Quant section 4 (15 questions) and section 5 (20 questions).
- gre-screen-reader-practice-test-3-quant.docx, gre-screen-reader-practice-test-3-answer-key.docx
  Practice Test 3: same test as the paper-delivered Practice Book. Sections 4 (15) and 5 (20).
- gre-sample-questions.pdf  https://www.ets.org/pdfs/gre/gre-sample-questions.pdf
  About 20 Quant sample questions with explanations. The same PDF also has the Verbal
  samples (3 Reading Comprehension, 2 Text Completion, 2 Sentence Equivalence), used in
  content/tests/gre-verbal/ets-sample.json.
- text/*.txt  plain-text dumps of the .docx files made with scripts/docx2txt.py.
  [IMG:media/imageN] marks an image in the .docx. Large images are figures;
  tiny ones are maths expressions, also given in words next to them.

Also free, not stored here
- GRE Math Review (exercises with answers): https://www.ets.org/pdfs/gre/gre-math-review.pdf
- Practice Book for the Paper-delivered GRE (same test as Practice Test 3, with % correct):
  https://www.ets.org/content/dam/ets-india/pdfs/gre/paper-delivered-test-practice-book.pdf
- POWERPREP: 2 free full adaptive tests, only on ets.org.
- gre-screen-reader-practice-test-1-verbal.docx, gre-screen-reader-practice-test-3-verbal.docx
  https://www.ets.org/content/dam/ets-org/pdfs/gre/  fetched 2026-11-02.
  Verbal sections 2 (15) and 3 (20) of each test. Answers are in the existing
  practice-test-N-answer-key files (Sections 2 and 3). Text dumps in text/.
